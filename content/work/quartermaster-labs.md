<p>I designed the complete visual identity for <strong>Quartermaster</strong>, a research firm that provides on-chain insights and market intelligence. The goal was to create a brand that feels analytical, timeless, and quietly confident.</p>
<p>Brand design, logo design, color system, typography, and illustration direction. The logo combines the letter <em>Q</em> with a compass needle, symbolizing guidance and direction. It's simple, balanced, and designed to reflect the firm's purpose of helping clients navigate complexity with precision.</p>
<h3>Typeface &amp; Colour</h3>
<p>The <strong>color palette</strong> leans into deep green, chosen for its sense of stability, trust, and understated sophistication. <strong>Merriweather</strong>, a classic serif, anchors the identity. Its scholarly tone brings a sense of credibility while staying approachable and readable across digital screens.</p>
<img alt="" src="https://framerusercontent.com/images/VNKkjtCpVZcuXAIofIjHA7xAd48.png">
<h3>Illustrations</h3>
<p>For the <strong>illustrations</strong>, I drew inspiration from Greek and Renaissance sculptures — figures that represent intellect and discipline. Using AI as a starting point, I refined each image to capture the feeling of carved precision and thoughtfulness, like ideas etched in marble.</p>
<img alt="" src="https://framerusercontent.com/images/MlddhpetVzgHEg072NNVmr76KPE.png">
<p>The result is a visual identity that feels measured, intelligent, and enduring. A brand built for thinkers who value clarity over noise.</p>
