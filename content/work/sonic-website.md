<h3>The Sonic Identity: Designing a Brand That Moves</h3>
<p>Some projects challenge your skills. Others shape who you are. Sonic was both.</p>
<p>When I took on the responsibility of designing Sonic's brand, it wasn't just about a logo or a color palette, it was about crafting a <strong>visual language</strong> that embodied movement, fluidity, and decentralization. A brand that didn't just sit still but propelled forward, much like the liquid staking engine it represented.</p>
<p>I wasn't alone in this. <strong>Rishav</strong>, our graphic designer, transformed my abstract ideas into tangible visuals. <strong>Mohak, our CEO</strong>, stood by every bold decision, giving me the creative freedom to push boundaries. Together, we set out to create something that felt alive.</p>

<h3>The Dragonfly: A Logo in Motion</h3>
<p>We started with a simple yet loaded question: <em>What does Sonic look like?</em></p>
<p>The answer wasn't just a wordmark. It was movement, speed, and connection. That's when the dragonfly emerged — a symbol of agility, transformation, and seamless exchange. The <strong>two halved circles</strong> within the wordmark visually represent the fluid motion of tokens within the staking ecosystem. Every curve, every space was intentional.</p>
<p>It wasn't just a logo; it was a <strong>philosophy of motion</strong> distilled into form.</p>
<img alt="" src="https://framerusercontent.com/images/XPCydMOyjr0nhHhEpwXASKO3c0M.png">

<h3>A New Visual Language</h3>
<p><strong>Illustrations:</strong><br>Sonic's visuals were built on <strong>simple geometric shapes</strong>, but what made them unique was the <strong>texture layered over them</strong>. This texture wasn't just an aesthetic choice, it symbolized the uniqueness of each staking transaction, reinforcing the idea that <strong>no two interactions on Sonic are the same</strong>.</p>
<img alt="" src="https://framerusercontent.com/images/JSZrCK2L2evBORy0uKTLFRTYoc.png">
<p><strong>Color Palette:</strong><br>We ditched the usual <strong>dark, cyberpunk-heavy Web3 aesthetic</strong>. Instead, we chose <strong>cool, refreshing tones</strong>, a breath of fresh air in the ecosystem. Sonic was different, and it had to <em>feel</em> different.</p>
<img alt="" src="https://framerusercontent.com/images/vzcqqmR07Q1RCqsZGSL4bRWsAtc.png">
<p><strong>Typography:</strong><br>Hauora Sans became the brand's voice, a <strong>neo-geometric sans</strong> that was clean, modern, and effortlessly readable. Its structure was both technical and human, reflecting Sonic's balance of <strong>engineering precision and user-first simplicity</strong>.</p>
<img alt="" src="https://framerusercontent.com/images/SlM0beR1F05dDVtLNJFF5zlnXI.png">

<h3>The Process: Learning, Leading, and Taking Risks</h3>
<p>This project wasn't just about creating a brand. It was about taking ownership. For the first time, I wasn't just executing, I was leading. I made the calls, set the direction, and trusted my instincts.</p>
<p>I also dove headfirst into <strong>new creative territories</strong>:</p>
<ul>
  <li><p>Designed my <strong>first-ever logo</strong> from scratch.</p></li>
  <li><p>Explored <strong>interactive 3D modeling</strong> for product visuals.</p></li>
  <li><p>Learned <strong>how to generate textures</strong> to add depth to illustrations.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>Every challenge became a lesson. Every risk taken was backed by <strong>Mohak's unwavering trust in creativity</strong>. And with <strong>Rishav's executional brilliance</strong>, my vision found its perfect form.</p>

<h3>The Takeaway: A Brand That Feels Alive</h3>
<p>Sonic wasn't just another liquid staking product, it was a vision brought to life through motion, clarity, and craft. We didn't stop at the brand. We designed the entire app experience and built the website — pieces that received genuine appreciation for their uniqueness and polish.</p>
<p>But not every bold experiment finds its market. Sonic, despite its momentum, didn't hit product-market fit. And that's okay. Because what we built still mattered.</p>
<p>It redefined how I think about design, not just as a function, but as a <strong>living, breathing story</strong> that earns trust, sparks emotion, and invites belief. Sonic taught me that great design is never wasted, it becomes part of who you are, shaping the next thing you create.</p>
