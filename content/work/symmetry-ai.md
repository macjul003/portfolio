<p>Symmetry AI was a project where I got to shape both the <strong>brand and the website</strong> from scratch. I started by defining the visual identity and then brought it to life through the website, building the entire experience in Framer. Along the way I also designed the careers page to help the team tell their story and attract the right people. It felt less like assembling pages and more like slowly giving the company a presence on the internet.</p>
