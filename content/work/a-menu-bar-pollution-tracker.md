<p>Checking air quality today means opening a website or an app. That breaks focus. On macOS, there's no way to see AQI by default in the menu bar.</p>
<p>I wanted air quality to be visible without doing anything.</p>
<p>So I built a small pollution tracker that lives in the menu bar. It shows air quality using color. You can see it at a glance while working.</p>
<p>There are no notifications, charts, or extra features. It just shows the current air quality.</p>
<p>Built with Antigravity.</p>
