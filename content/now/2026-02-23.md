## Photo map: thumbnail markers

Replaced generic circle markers with actual photo thumbnails on the map. Clusters now show a representative photo with a count overlay instead of a plain number badge. The map became a lot more interesting to scroll around once you could see the photos directly on it.

Added 14 new geotagged photos — India, Singapore, Thailand, UAE, Philippines. Two HEIC files didn't yield GPS data which was a useful reminder that not all phone photos embed location even when location services are on.
